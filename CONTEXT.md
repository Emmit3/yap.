# Yap research and short-video creation

Yap helps creators turn an idea, public creative research, and their own material into an original short video. Business promos are one supported use case within this entertainment and media workflow.

## Language

**Investigation**:
A saved research question, its collected public evidence, derived analysis, and the creator's notes.
_Avoid_: Surveillance session

**Evidence**:
An attributed public source record with its collection provenance and known publication details.
_Avoid_: Verified fact, generated finding

**Observed connection**:
A public interaction reported by a source, such as a reply, repost, or mention.
_Avoid_: Inferred relationship, follower graph

**Content relationship**:
A derived link between records whose wording or topics are similar.
_Avoid_: Observed interaction

**Research direction**:
Selected findings and notes that inform creative choices while retaining source attribution and cautions.
_Avoid_: Product facts, automatic approval

**Workflow**:
A creator's ongoing video conversation, supplied photos, brief, and current creative material.
_Avoid_: Chat session, project draft

**Brief**:
The product, audience, message, and desired customer action that guide a promo.
_Avoid_: Prompt specification

**Refinement question**:
A focused question that helps the creator describe what to highlight or change.
_Avoid_: Required onboarding step

**Script**:
The proposed words and their timing for a promo.
_Avoid_: Finished video

**Scene**:
A timed visual direction tied to a script segment and supplied photos.
_Avoid_: Generated footage

**Verification**:
Checks that a brief, script, or set of scenes is complete, consistent, and based on current material.
_Avoid_: Fact checking, approval

**Approval**:
The creator's explicit acceptance of the current creative material for production.
_Avoid_: Automatic verification

**Render**:
The production of a downloadable video from approved scenes and actual supplied photos.
_Avoid_: Planning export
