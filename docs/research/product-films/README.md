# Product film references

103 distinct finished product-film references across 87 brand labels. These are linked creator campaigns and explicitly labeled spec work, rather than stock-footage items.

Open the [local catalogue](http://127.0.0.1:5173/showcase/film-references.html), or consume [the public JSON](../../../public/film-references.json).

## How this list was qualified

- Each counted item has a named product and a primary creator, brand, or creator-owned film page.
- The source exposes an actual film/player/full-video source; still-only galleries and text-only film claims are outside the count. Player presence is evidence of a film, not a claim that every external player was played end to end.
- One campaign counts once even when its case contains several cuts or deliverables. Identical primary case pages are deduplicated.
- Techniques come from creator descriptions. Why-it-fits notes are editorial interpretations, not verified business results. Unknown film years remain null; a film date does not establish a product launch date.
- 3 items are explicitly labeled spec work: Milia Matcha — Floraison / Cérémonie, Nuii at Its Most Tempting, Tasty Cleaning.
- Four older ABC Vimeo embeds are held outside the public list because current watch availability could not be established. Their source evidence remains in the additional research notes.
