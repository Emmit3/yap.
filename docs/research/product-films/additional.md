# Additional product-film research

Fifteen primary creator cases were investigated. Four older embedded films are currently unavailable and are excluded from the public film count: OPKIX, Rare Metal, Nixon x Star Wars, and Xtep Reactive Coil. Their original public players return unavailable responses; the underlying cause is unconfirmed. Three other cases overlap the lifestyle research and are deduplicated in the public catalogue. Techniques come from source descriptions; fit notes are editorial interpretations, not claims of end-to-end playback. Videos remain on their creators' original sites.
