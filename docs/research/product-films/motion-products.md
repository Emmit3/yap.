# Finished product films: motion, sport, travel and mobility

Verified 2026-11-14. This list contains **30 distinct product campaign cases across 25 brands**. Series, cutdowns, feature variants and behind-the-scenes material are counted once per campaign. These are product campaign videos and creator cuts, rather than stock footage, generic showreels or image-only identities. A longer product overview and a campaign montage are explicitly identified below so they are not mistaken for standalone short hype spots.

Primary studio, credited director and brand-specific creator film pages were discovered with Firecrawl search and read with Firecrawl scrape. Every counted entry has an actual player/video element on its primary page or is the credited creator's exact film page. Official film text alone does not qualify for the JSON. This does **not** mean that every stream was playback-tested: several Mux/FrameRate players reported loading or network errors in text retrieval. Their source pages remain available as watch links. No media was downloaded, rehosted, or added to the application.

Year is recorded only when primary project or film text explicitly identifies it; an upload-directory date, copyright footer, product-model suffix, or relative timestamp does not establish a campaign year. Exact upload dates are useful for matching films but are not automatically promoted to release years. Unknown years remain null in [the JSON array](./motion-products.json). Production techniques are source-supported. Each whyItFits field is explicitly a curator inference.

## Cold audit: naming, version and player scope

- [Mercurial's current case](https://mvsm.com/project/mercurial) combines World Cup 2014 with a generation suffix that is not retained here. Its [creator-uploaded film](https://vimeo.com/95755233) is named Nike ~ Mercurial Superfly and explicitly dates its director's cut to 2014. The record therefore keeps 2014 and leaves the boot generation unspecified.
- The [S25 case](https://futuredeluxe.com/work/arcteryx-s25) includes more than one shoe, but [the selected film](https://vimeo.com/1079812736) is titled Arc'teryx Vertex. Likewise, [Run Valley](https://futuredeluxe.com/work/diadora-run-valley) covers multiple shoes while [the selected film](https://vimeo.com/918388030) is Diadora_Cellula. Both records are narrowed to the actual linked shoe.
- [Merrell's selected film](https://vimeo.com/1210469473) is a director's cut. [Dacia's selected film](https://vimeo.com/989581108) is an aerodynamics capsule. [Genesis's selected film](https://vimeo.com/1029623879) is a product-range campaign montage. These are identified explicitly rather than treated as unnamed full-campaign films.
- The [INFINITI case](https://futuredeluxe.com/work/infiniti) embeds a [working 0:52 player](https://player.vimeo.com/video/1114837456) titled INFINITI - QX65 Monograph (FD Edit). The bare Vimeo film page returns 404, so the verified embedded-player URL is retained.
- [Specialized's exact linked film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3R3cocNro6A) is a 4:30 product-and-technology overview on Specialized University, uploaded in 2021. It is a lower-fit explanation reference, not represented as a short hype spot. [Fenomeno](https://www.fenomeno.be/portfolio/specialized-turbo-tero) provides its local-production credits.
- [Tonal's case](https://www.iamgraphicartist.com/portfolio/work/tonal) includes a Main Film heading and development material. Its FrameRate embed does not independently name the individual cut, so the watch URL remains the contextual case page.
- [Lululemon Director's Cut](https://www.iamgraphicartist.com/portfolio/work/lululemondc) identifies footwear but does not name its shoe model on the credited director's page. The JSON deliberately leaves that model unspecified.
- [Mutant Bikes 2022](https://vimeo.com/676223701) is the exact creator-uploaded film page: Inertia Studios is the uploader and credited design, direction and production studio.
- [Nike Flyknit](https://mvsm.com/project/flyknit) includes a motion identity but qualifies through its separately embedded cinematic product-technology film. Identity assets are not counted as additional films.

A campaign's technical or performance claims are reported as its creator's presentation; this research does not independently validate the products' engineering claims. The separate JSON retains product, brand, creator, category, campaign/spec status, source and watch URLs, year where known, techniques, grounded evidence, player evidence and verification time for every counted entry.
