# Finished product films: lifestyle research

Verified 2026-11-14. This catalogue contains **33 distinct campaign/spec cases**: 30 commissioned campaign cases and 3 explicitly identified self-initiated/spec cases. A multi-film campaign is counted once. The JSON contains product-focused films with an actual main player or an official watch link on a primary maker, director, or brand page. General studio reels, still galleries and text-only/unavailable leads are excluded from that count.

This is a source and player-evidence review, without browser playback or video downloads. Techniques below come from the cited maker descriptions or production credits; selection rationales are marked as curator inference. Years remain null unless the source explicitly establishes the year.
